# AICredits Cookbook Project Ideas

## 1. WhatsApp Order-Taking Bot For A Kirana Store

**Problem Statement:** Build a chat assistant that collects grocery orders, confirms quantities, and prepares a clean order summary for the shopkeeper.

**What You'll Learn:** Function calling, conversation state, and per-customer cost control.

**Suggested Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Why AICredits Helps Here:** Per-key budget caps can help keep each customer or bot deployment under control.

## 2. UPI Expense Categorizer

**Problem Statement:** Parse bank SMS messages or statement rows into categories such as food, rent, travel, utilities, and shopping.

**What You'll Learn:** Structured outputs and batch pricing.

**Suggested Difficulty:** Beginner

**Why AICredits Helps Here:** INR billing makes small batch experiments easier for Indian students and developers.

## 3. Regional-Language Complaint Summarizer

**Problem Statement:** Convert customer complaint audio into short summaries and action items in the language a support team can review.

**What You'll Learn:** Audio pipelines and Indic languages.

**Suggested Difficulty:** Advanced

**Why AICredits Helps Here:** Access to many models lets you compare language quality across providers.

## 4. Resume JD Matcher With Explanations

**Full Recipe:** [recipes/resume-jd-matcher](recipes/resume-jd-matcher)

**Problem Statement:** Compare a candidate resume with a job description and produce a match score with evidence-backed strengths and gaps.

**What You'll Learn:** Embeddings, reranking, and hallucination control.

**Suggested Difficulty:** Beginner

**Why AICredits Helps Here:** Cheap INR billing makes repeated prompt and scoring experiments practical.

## 5. College Notes RAG Bot

**Problem Statement:** Let students ask questions over class notes and receive answers with citations back to the source notes.

**What You'll Learn:** Chunking, vector search, and citation.

**Suggested Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Why AICredits Helps Here:** One gateway makes it easier to compare answer quality across model families.

## 6. News/Legal Document Simplifier

**Problem Statement:** Simplify dense English news or legal text into Hindi with a streaming reading experience.

**What You'll Learn:** Translation evals and streaming UX.

**Suggested Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Why AICredits Helps Here:** 300+ models give you options for quality, latency, and cost comparison.

## 7. Customer-Support Autoresponder With Human Handoff

**Problem Statement:** Draft support replies, detect uncertain cases, and hand complex tickets to a human agent.

**What You'll Learn:** Guardrails and confidence thresholds.

**Suggested Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Why AICredits Helps Here:** Per-key budgets can limit spend per support queue or experiment.

## 8. AI Code-Review GitHub Action On A Budget

**Problem Statement:** Review pull requests automatically and comment only on high-confidence issues to control API usage.

**What You'll Learn:** API keys with per-key budget caps.

**Suggested Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Why AICredits Helps Here:** Per-key controls help isolate CI spend from other projects.

## 9. Voice IVR Replacement For Appointment Booking

**Problem Statement:** Build a voice flow that listens to callers, extracts appointment details, and confirms bookings.

**What You'll Learn:** STT/TTS loops and latency budgets.

**Suggested Difficulty:** Advanced

**Why AICredits Helps Here:** A single gateway helps compare models while tuning speed and cost.

## 10. Price-Comparison Shopping Agent

**Problem Statement:** Search multiple stores for a product, compare prices, and explain tradeoffs such as delivery time or seller reliability.

**What You'll Learn:** Tool use, scraping etiquette, and caching to cut cost.

**Suggested Difficulty:** Advanced

**Why AICredits Helps Here:** Model comparison helps reserve stronger models only for harder reasoning steps.

**Full Recipe:** [recipes/exam-mcq-generator](recipes/exam-mcq-generator)

**Problem Statement:** Generate multiple-choice questions from source text and evaluate a student's submitted answers against the answer key.

**What You'll Learn:** Rubric prompting and batch generation.

**Suggested Difficulty:** Beginner

**Why AICredits Helps Here:** INR prepaid credits make classroom-scale experiments easier to budget.

## 12. Multi-Model Eval Harness

**Full Recipe:** [recipes/model-eval-harness](recipes/model-eval-harness)

**Problem Statement:** Run one task across several models and compare latency, token usage, output length, and approximate relative cost.
